# Treatment: Leniwa Kluska · Plakat (A)

15 s · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 60 fps · 144 BPM (1 beat = 25 frames, 36 beats = 15.000 s) · no narration · Polish type.

**Language:** kinetic type + montage (library/languages/kinetic-type.md), the poster variant. Barlow Condensed 900 caps (the app's own display face), brand lime #C3F037 / orange #FF5B24 / ink #0E0F11, the words end in a full stop (the brand's shape: the dot becomes the flood at the drop).
**Route:** hybrid. A four-word spine (one bar per word), one montage burst of real full-bleed screens on beats, a stop, the drop, a payoff, the mark.
**Message left behind:** one app does the training, the running, the food and the winning.
**Music world:** trap, half-time (music.md §7). 808 kick + sub, clap on beat 3, rolling hats, a bell motif G–B♭–D–G (rises: "lightweight, baby") that returns as the sonic logo. Gm, own score, `!hit!` on the drop. Chosen for sport / bold; not offered to the user.
**Departures from the references:** the stop is 2 beats (0.83 s) not 1.6 s because the film is 15 s; the burst starts at 44 % rather than 30–50 % for the same reason.

## Continuity chain

TRENUJ. phone → (cut on beat) orange field with the same phone slab sliding up → (cut) lime field, the card crop → (cut) black field → burst of full-bleed screens (the screen edges are the carried shape; every cut on a beat) → stop: the full stop of the last word is the dot → the dot grows into the lime flood (drop) → the phone and its tab bar → the orange + button of the tab bar → the orange disc grows to the centre → the app icon → wordmark. The cuts inside the spine are on the beat, not carried; the stop → drop and tab bar → icon links are carried.
